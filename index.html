About the studio

Sawasdeeka began as one treatment room above a florist on the Prinsengracht. Eleven years later it is six therapists, all clinically trained, all still working to the same rule: one body at a time, unhurried.

We keep a single appointment on the hour so the room is never rushed between clients, and we never upsell from the table.
